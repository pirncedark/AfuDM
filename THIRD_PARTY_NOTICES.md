# Third-party software / Üçüncü taraf yazılımlar

AfuDM bundles or downloads the following programs. Each keeps its own license.
AfuDM aşağıdaki programları paketinde taşır veya istek üzerine indirir.
Her biri kendi lisansına tabidir.

## Bundled in every package / Her pakette

### aria2 1.37.0 — `engine/aria2c.exe`
- License: GNU General Public License v2.0 or later (with OpenSSL exception)
- Source code: https://github.com/aria2/aria2/tree/release-1.37.0
- License text: https://github.com/aria2/aria2/blob/release-1.37.0/COPYING
- The binary is the unmodified official Windows build.

## Downloaded on demand (Settings > Engines) / İstek üzerine indirilir

### yt-dlp — `engine/yt-dlp.exe`
- License: The Unlicense (public domain)
- Source code: https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp
